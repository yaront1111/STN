SRTM Synthetic Terrain Data
============================

Generated for GPS-Free Navigation System Testing

Coverage:
  Region: Western United States (demo)
  Latitude: 30°N to 40°N
  Longitude: 120°W to 110°W

Resolution:
  Spatial: 1 arc-minute (simplified from 1 arc-second)
  Vertical: 1 meter

Data Format:
  Type: Binary float32
  Units: Meters above EGM96 geoid
  No-data value: -32768

Features:
  - Pacific Ocean depths
  - Sierra Nevada mountains
  - Rocky Mountains
  - Central Valley
  - Realistic terrain roughness
