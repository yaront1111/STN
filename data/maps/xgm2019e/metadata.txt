XGM2019e Synthetic Data
=======================

Generated for GPS-Free Navigation System Testing

Grid Parameters:
  Resolution: 1 degrees
  Latitude points: 181 (-90 to +90)
  Longitude points: 361 (-180 to +180)

Data Files:
  coefficients.dat - Spherical harmonic coefficients
  anomaly_1arcmin.dat - Gravity anomaly grid (mGal)
  gradient_tensor.dat - Gravity gradient tensor grid (Eotvos)

Reference System:
  Ellipsoid: WGS84
  Tide System: Tide-free
  Units: mGal (anomaly), Eotvos (gradients)
